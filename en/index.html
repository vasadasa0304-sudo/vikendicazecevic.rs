A-frame cabin · 60 m²

A mountain stay with a view

Zečević Cabin is a mountain A-frame holiday cabin in Zlatar, suitable for up to 6 guests. The ground floor includes a fully equipped kitchen with a dining area, a living room with a pull-out corner sofa, one bedroom with two beds, and a bathroom. Upstairs, there is a double bed, making the cabin practical for families, couples, or small groups.

From the living room, guests can step out onto a spacious terrace overlooking the natural surroundings of Zlatar. The terrace is one of the cabin’s main highlights — ideal for morning coffee, daytime relaxation, or a quiet evening in the mountains.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How many guests can stay at the cabin?

The cabin accommodates up to 6 guests.

How many rooms, beds and bathrooms are there?

The cabin has 2 rooms and 5 beds in total — a ground-floor bedroom with two beds and a double bed upstairs — plus 1 bathroom.

How big is the cabin?

It is an A-frame cabin of approximately 60 m².

What is the altitude?

The cabin sits at approximately 1,200 metres above sea level, on Mount Zlatar.

Is there Wi-Fi, parking and a barbecue?

Yes. Wi-Fi internet, free parking and a barbecue are available to guests.

Is the cabin heated in winter?

Yes. The cabin has electric heating and Norwegian radiators, as well as air conditioning.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is after 14:00 and check-out is before 10:00.

What is nearby?

The Zlatar Rehabilitation Centre, sports courts, fitness trails and walking paths are all close by.

How do I book and how much does it cost?

The price depends on the dates and number of guests. Send an inquiry through the site or contact us by phone or email and we will reply with an exact offer.
